The solution

One plugin the agent calls to pay. It never holds a key or a card.

01
Verify
Mandate, page vs. request, hidden instructions, merchant trust score.
02
Decide
Approved · Held for a human · Blocked. Deterministic rules, no randomness.
03
Execute
Single-use virtual card in EUR, capped and merchant-locked. Or AVAX.
04
Prove
Every decision, even refusals, anchored on Avalanche. Kill switch pauses everything.

Money stays in the customer's bank. PayGuard is a technical provider, not a payment institution.
